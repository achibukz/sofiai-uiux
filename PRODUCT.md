# GIA — Generative Influencer Analyst

## Product Purpose

GIA is a web analytics product by SOFI AI. A Filipino TikTok creator pastes their profile link and receives a plain-language report: hook scores, audience signals, sentiment breakdown, and specific post recommendations. The pitch is "understand why your TikToks land — or don't" without needing to speak analytics.

## Register

brand (landing / marketing surface) + product (onboarding flow + report dashboard)

Primary surface for this submission: landing page redesign + full flow prototype.

## Users

Filipino TikTok micro-creators (1k–100k followers). They see their metrics but don't understand them. They are not data-literate, but they are digital-native and have high aesthetic standards — they consume premium content daily. They judge tools by how professional and trustworthy they look. A product that looks cheap loses them immediately.

Secondary: SOFI AI hiring panel evaluating the application. They care about design thinking and clear UX communication as much as the finished product.

## Brand voice

"Creator coach with grounded data." Authoritative but not clinical. Personality without being childish. The current landing at https://sofi-ai-gia.netlify.app/ is playful-only — this redesign lifts it to premium-playful hybrid: editorial structure with moments of warmth and wit.

## Tone words

Grounded. Sharp. Warm.

Not: clinical, corporate, generic-SaaS, bubbly, cartoon-adjacent.

## Anti-references

- The current sofi-ai-gia.netlify.app landing (playful-only, no premium weight)
- Generic SaaS dashboards with dark-navy-blue hero + floating cards
- TikTok's own analytics UI (flat, functional, no personality)
- "AI startup" landing clichés: gradient text, hero-metric layouts, glassmorphism cards

## Strategic principles

1. Dashboard beats PDF — the redesign replaces the PDF report with an interactive web dashboard. This is the product's strongest design argument.
2. Inputs signal intelligence — the onboarding asks for TikTok link + content goals + niche + optional competitor handles. More signal = more credible output.
3. The Filipino creator context is the differentiator — this is not a generic analytics tool. Speak to the user's reality.
4. Every screen should look intentional — this is a job application. Every pixel is evaluated.
